Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Summary

Calculator inputs stay in your browser. We collect personal information only when you choose to submit a form (pay review, contact, contest, application, pledge or newsletter). We use cookies for site function, analytics and advertising.

Information we collect

  • Information you submit: name, email, optional phone, and the details you type into a form.
  • Automatic information: standard log data and cookie identifiers collected by our host and by third parties such as Google.
  • Local storage: your theme and cookie choices, stored only in your browser.

How we use it

To respond to you, deliver the pay review, run contests, process pledges, consider applications, send newsletters you requested, improve the site and show ads. We introduce you to a partner only if you explicitly opt in. We do not sell your personal information.

Form processing

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox through a third-party form-forwarding service (FormSubmit). That service processes the submission solely to deliver it.

Cookies and advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Choosing “Essential only” in our cookie notice requests non-personalised ads.

YouTube videos load in privacy-enhanced mode and only after you click play.

Your choices and rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of personal information you submitted by contacting us. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA/UK under GDPR, California under CCPA/CPRA, or Canada under PIPEDA), you may have additional rights, which we honour.

Retention and security

We keep submissions only as long as needed for the purpose you submitted them, and use reasonable safeguards. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 where applicable), and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We will post updates here with a new effective date.
