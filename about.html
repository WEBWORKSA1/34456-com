About 34456.com

We turn the numbers that run your life — salary, take-home pay, loans and savings — into answers you can act on in seconds.

Our mission

Most people make their biggest money decisions with the least information: an offer letter, a paycheck stub, a loan quote. 34456.com exists to give everyone the same clarity a finance team gives a company — free, fast and private.

Why the name?

Read as “34 456,” our name is a real number: $34,456 a year is about $16.57 an hour, a salary millions of people earn, offer or negotiate. It reminds us who we build for. The full story →

How we build and check our tools

  • Tax amounts come from official IRS and Canada Revenue Agency publications and are updated each year.
  • Every formula is shown on its page so you can verify it.
  • Calculations run in your browser; we do not store what you type.
  • Guides are reviewed for accuracy and dated when updated.

How we make money

Display advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships, optional partner introductions from our Free Pay Review, and reader support. None of these change calculator results.

Corrections

Spot an error? Tell us — we fix verified errors quickly and note material corrections on the page.
