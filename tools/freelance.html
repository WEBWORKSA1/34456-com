Why freelancers must charge more than employees

Employees get paid holidays, employer payroll taxes, benefits and someone else's equipment. Freelancers pay self-employment tax (15.3% in the US on most net earnings), buy their own tools and insurance, and spend 20–40% of their time on sales, admin and learning that nobody pays for. That is why a freelancer typically needs 1.5–2× the equivalent employee hourly rate.

Quote by project or day where you can: clients compare outcomes, not hours, and you keep the upside of working efficiently.

Frequently asked questions

How many billable hours are realistic?

Most full-time freelancers bill 20–30 hours a week; the rest goes to sales, admin and learning.

Should I show my hourly rate?

Many freelancers keep the hourly rate internal and quote fixed project prices built from it.
