34456 by the numbers

Thirty-four thousand, four hundred and fifty-six is an even, composite, deficient number: its prime factorization is 2³ × 59 × 73, it has 16 divisors, and those proper divisors add up to 32,144. In binary it is 1000011010011000, in hexadecimal 0x8698, and its digits sum to 22 (digital root 4). As an annual salary it equals about $16.57 an hour — read why that matters.

Frequently asked questions

What is a deficient number?

A number whose proper divisors add up to less than the number itself. 34456’s proper divisors sum to 32,144, so it is deficient.

How large a number can I enter?

Up to one trillion. Factorisation runs in your browser in milliseconds.
