Why “true hourly value” beats salary

Two offers with the same salary can differ by thousands once you price in the retirement match, health benefits, bonus and — most often ignored — the hours you spend commuting and the days you don't have to work. This tool counts commute time across 48 working weeks and subtracts paid days off from 260 weekdays, then divides total compensation by your real hours.

Use it before you negotiate: if Offer B wins on base but loses on hourly value, ask Offer B for a remote day or extra PTO rather than only more salary.

Frequently asked questions

How do I value health benefits?

Use the employer’s share of premiums from the offer letter or benefits guide. If you don’t know, many employer plans are worth several thousand dollars a year for single coverage.

Should I include equity?

Add only the vested value you are reasonably confident in, divided by the vesting years, to the bonus field.
