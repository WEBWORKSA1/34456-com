How to use these tools together

  1. Start with your pay. Convert your salary or wage with the Salary ↔ Hourly converter.
  2. See what you keep. Run the result through Take-Home Pay to subtract income tax and payroll contributions.
  3. Give every dollar a job. Plug your monthly net into the 50/30/20 Budget.
  4. Grow the gap. Use Compound Interest to see what your savings rate becomes in 10–30 years.
  5. Earn more. Compare offers with Job Offer Compare and ask with the Raise Script Builder.
