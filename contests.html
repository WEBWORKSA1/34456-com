This round's themes

  1. Best money tip — one habit that saves or earns $500+ a year.
  2. Salary win story — how you negotiated a raise or better offer (numbers welcome, names optional).
  3. Guess the number — what is $34,456 a year per hour after US federal tax and FICA (single, 2,080 hours)? Closest answer to the cent wins the tie-breaker.

How winners are chosen

Entries are scored by the 34456.com editorial team on usefulness (40%), originality (30%), clarity (20%) and use of real numbers (10%). “Guess the number” is decided by the closest correct calculation, with earliest entry breaking ties.

Official rules (summary)
