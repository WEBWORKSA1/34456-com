Salary vs. Hourly Pay: Which Really Pays More?

How to compare a salaried offer with an hourly one: the 2,080-hour rule, overtime law, paid time off and the hidden hours that change the answer.

By the 34456.com Editorial Team · Fact-checked against IRS and CRA publications ·

The 2,080-hour rule

The standard way to convert between salary and hourly pay assumes 40 hours a week for 52 weeks — 2,080 hours. A $34,456 salary therefore equals $16.57 an hour, and a $20 hourly wage equals $41,600 a year. Recruiters, payroll systems and government pay tables all use this shortcut. It is a good starting point, but it is not your real hourly rate.

Three things the shortcut ignores

  1. Paid time off. With 11 holidays and 10 vacation days you work 239 days, not 260. A salaried worker effectively earns about 9% more per hour worked than the headline rate.
  2. Unpaid extra hours. If a salaried job routinely runs 50 hours a week, $34,456 becomes $13.25 an hour. Hourly non-exempt workers in the US must generally be paid 1.5× for hours over 40 in a workweek under the Fair Labor Standards Act.
  3. Benefits. Salaried roles more often include health coverage, retirement matching and paid sick leave. These can add 20–30% to the value of compensation.

When hourly pays more

Hourly wins when overtime is frequent and paid, when you value flexibility to pick up or drop shifts, or when the salaried role expects long weeks without extra pay. A $19/hour job with five hours of weekly overtime pays about $45,600 a year — well above a $40,000 salary.

When salary pays more

Salary wins when hours are genuinely stable, benefits are strong and the role has a clear raise and promotion path. Salaried positions also smooth income, which makes it easier to qualify for loans and plan a budget.

A five-minute comparison method

  1. Convert both offers to annual pay with the Salary ↔ Hourly converter.
  2. Add bonus, retirement match and the employer's share of benefits.
  3. Estimate real weekly hours including commute.
  4. Run both through Job Offer Compare to get the true hourly value.
  5. Use the gap as leverage — ask the weaker offer to close it.

Frequently asked questions

Is a salaried job exempt from overtime?

Not automatically. In the US, exemption depends on both a salary threshold and job duties. Many salaried employees are still entitled to overtime.

How do I convert hourly to salary?

Multiply the hourly rate by weekly hours and by 52. $20 × 40 × 52 = $41,600.
